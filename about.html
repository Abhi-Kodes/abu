About Ram Krishan Industries

India's trusted manufacturer of entrance automation, industrial doors, rolling shutters, and high-speed door solutions — built for quality, safety, and long-lasting performance.

Who We Are

We are in the market since 1956 and have been considered one of the most reliable brands since our inception offering clients world-class rolling shutters, doors, grills and other security systems.

Ram Krishan Industries stands as a recognised brand, a testament to our dedication, innovation and unwavering focus on delivering quality and dependable customer support.

Our product portfolio includes a wide range of rolling shutters, sliding doors & gates, high-speed doors, fire-rated rolling shutters, and our in-house American Eagle Motors known for their superior performance and durability.

Continuing our legacy of innovation, we are also introducing advanced solutions like dock levelers, sectional doors, garage doors and various customised security systems to meet evolving industry needs.

Manufacturing

Precision Manufacturing at Our Core

Our manufacturing facility in Mayapuri Industrial Area, New Delhi, features state-of-the-art roll-forming machinery, automated CNC cutting stations, precision welding setups, and specialized assembly bays to ensure strict quality standards for every door and shutter.

Using certified raw materials — premium galvanised steel, aircraft-grade aluminium, heavy-duty polycarbonate, and high-density PUF insulation — our experienced engineers produce entrance systems designed to withstand intensive industrial usage and tough Indian weather conditions.
